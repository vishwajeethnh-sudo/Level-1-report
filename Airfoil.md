## Task 2: Airfoil (Surface modelling)
### Objective
The objective of this task is to design a simple aerofoil for an HTOL aircraft using Fusion 360 and understand the nomenclature and basic geometry of a NACA aerofoil profile.

### Procedure
An airfoil is a wing which is a major aviation component used in an Aircraft. the design of the airfoil is developed by National Advisory Committee for Aeronautics (NACA). Each airfoil has its own unique number. The parameters in the numerical code can be entered into equations to precisely generate the cross-section of the airfoil and calculate its properties. The digits in the code determine the **camber lines, maximum thickness, and special nose features**. 

I studied the basic geometry of an airfoil, including the leading edge, trailing edge, chord line, and camber line. I reviewed the NACA profile designation from suitable website and created CSV file and copied the values for xyz coordinates of the airfoil profile. I imported the values of xyz coordinates in Solidworks in the insert curves section through reference point section, created the airfoil 2D design. I then developed the 3D model and checked its overall geometry.

### Learning

This task helped me understand what an Airfoil is and how is it designed for precise calculations. I also learnt the relationship between airfoil geometry and CAD modelling. Additionally the meaning of the main airfoil terms and gained an introduction to the use of CAD in aircraft design.
