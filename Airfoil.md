## Task 2: Airfoil (Surface modelling)
### Objective
The objective of this task is to design a simple aerofoil for an HTOL aircraft using Fusion 360 and understand the nomenclature and basic geometry of a NACA aerofoil profile.

### Procedure
I studied the basic geometry of an aerofoil, including the leading edge, trailing edge, chord line, and camber line. I reviewed the NACA profile designation from suitable website and created CSV file and copied the values for xyz coordinates of the airfoil profile. Then in Solidworks I imported the values of xyz coordinates in the insert curves section through reference point section, created the airfoil 2D design. I then developed the 3D model and checked its overall geometry.

### Learning

This task helped me understand the relationship between aerofoil geometry and CAD modelling. I also learnt the meaning of the main aerofoil terms and gained an introduction to the use of CAD in aircraft design.
