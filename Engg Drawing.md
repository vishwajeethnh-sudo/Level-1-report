## Introduction to Engineering Drawing
### Objective

The objective of this task is to understand isometric and orthographic projections and represent three-dimensional objects through engineering drawings. Also creating 2D CAD drawings from the reference designs and learn hoe to draw a Level 2 Sierpinski triangle on a drawing sheet with clearly labelled dimensions. 

### Procedure

I used a mini drafter to draw at least four models based on the provided examples. I also referred to the supplied material and prepared seven orthographic projections and seven isometric projections as required by the task.

For the 2D drawing in CAD I referred to the designated folder containing the reference drawings and recreated the required designs in the Sketcher workspace using the drawing tools. I checked the sketches against the references to ensure that the geometry was represented correctly.

I constructed the Sierpinski triangle by starting with a triangle and subdividing it into smaller triangular sections according to the Level 2 pattern. I then prepared a drawing sheet and labelled the required dimensions neatly.

### Learning

This task helped me understand how different views represent an object and how orthographic and isometric drawings are used to communicate the shape and dimensions of mechanical components. Also helped me become familiar with 2D sketching in CAD and understand how reference drawings can be recreated digitally.
