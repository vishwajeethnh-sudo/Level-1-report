## Task 1
### Objective

The objective of this task is to understand isometric and orthographic projections and represent three-dimensional objects through engineering drawings.

### Procedure

I used a mini drafter to draw at least four models based on the provided examples. I also referred to the supplied material and prepared seven orthographic projections and seven isometric projections as required by the task.

### Learning

This task helped me understand how different views represent an object and how orthographic and isometric drawings are used to communicate the shape and dimensions of mechanical components.
