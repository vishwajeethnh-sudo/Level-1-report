## Task 2: Hollow Vase (Organic Designing Using Forms)

### Objective
The objective of this task is to design a hollow vase for MARVEL using the Forms feature in Fusion 360.

### Procedure
I used the Forms environment to create and refine the vase's organic shape. I modelled the hollow interior and checked the design against the specified base, aperture, and wall-thickness parameters.

### Learning
This task helped me understand freeform modelling and how to create smooth, curved shapes in Fusion 360 while maintaining the required dimensions and wall thickness.
