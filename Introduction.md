# Mechanical Tasks
## Introduction To Designing 
### *Fundamentals of Design*
Design and Prototyping is an essential part of mechanical engineering, connecting theoretical engineering concepts with practical design and manufacturing applications. Computer-Aided Design (CAD) software allows engineers to create accurate models, visualize components, develop assemblies, and evaluate the functionality of mechanical systems before manufacturing them.

*Computer Aided Design (CAD)*

Computer-Aided Design is the use of computer software to create, modify, analyse, and document engineering designs.

CAD is widely used in mechanical engineering for designing machine components, automotive parts, industrial equipment, product enclosures, and manufacturing assemblies.

Autodesk Fusion 360 is used in the Mechanical Design module to develop 2D sketches, 3D models, assemblies, and mechanical animations.
