## Task 2: Marvel Postbox (Sheet Metal)
### Objective
The objective of this task is to design a postbox for MARVEL using the sheet metal environment in Fusion 360.

### Procedure
I designed the postbox using sheet metal tools and considered its base, walls, bends, flanges, and access opening. I followed the specified dimensions and considered usability, compatibility, creativity, and optimisation in the design.

Parameters

Thickness: 1 mm

Height: 1 ft ~ 308mm

### Learning
This task helped me understand sheet metal modelling and the importance of material thickness, bends, and manufacturability when designing a practical product.
