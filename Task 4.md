## Task 4 Assembly and Animation
### Objective
The objective of this task is to design a mechanical mechanism from scratch, assemble its components, and animate its movement in CAD software.

### Procedure
I selected 2 of the mechanisms provided in the resources: a simple slider mechanism, quick-return mechanism, Geneva wheel mechanism, or ratchet and pawl mechanism. I modelled the required components for quick return mechanism and the geneva wheel, assembled them using appropriate joints, and used the animation tools to demonstrate the mechanism's movement.

### Learning
This task helped me understand mechanical assemblies, joint selection, and the basic kinematics of mechanisms. It also showed how animation can be used to visualise the movement of a mechanical system.
