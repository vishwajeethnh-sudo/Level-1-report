## Task 5: Spur Gear Design
### Objective

The objective of this task is to design a pair of spur gears in Fusion 360, calculate the relevant gear parameters, assemble the gears, and animate their motion.

### Procedure

I studied the basic working principle of spur gears and the relationship between the number of teeth, module, pitch diameter, and gear ratio. I created a 20-tooth input gear and a 40-tooth output gear using the specified module and pressure angle. I calculated the pitch diameters and centre distance, positioned the gears, and prepared an animation to demonstrate their meshing motion.

**Specifications**

Gear ratio: 2:1

Input gear: 20 teeth

Output gear: 40 teeth

Module: 3 mm

Pressure angle: 20°

**Calculations**

Pitch diameter = Module × Number of teeth

Input gear pitch diameter = 3 × 20 = 60 mm

Output gear pitch diameter = 3 × 40 = 120 mm

Centre distance = (60 + 120) / 2 = 90 mm

Gear ratio = 40 / 20 = 2:1

### Learning

This task helped me connect gear theory with CAD modelling. I learnt how to calculate pitch diameters and centre distance, understand the relationship between gear sizes and rotational speed, and use an assembly animation to demonstrate motion transfer.
