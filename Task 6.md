## Task 6: Parametric Box
### Objective

The objective of this task is to design a parametric box in Fusion 360 with threaded features and different lid mechanisms, including snap joints and sliding joints.

### Procedure
I created the basic box geometry and organised the key dimensions as parameters so that the design could be modified efficiently. I then worked on different lid designs, including a snap-fit lid, and a sliding lid. I assembled the parts and used animation to demonstrate how the different lids fit and move.

### Learning
This task helped me understand parametric modelling and the use of dimensions and relationships to control a design. I also learnt about different joining mechanisms and the importance of fit, clearances, and assembly motion in product design.
