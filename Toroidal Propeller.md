## Task 2: Toroidal Propeller (Surface Modelling)
### Objective
The objective of this task is to understand the purpose of a toroidal propeller and design one in CAD software that is compatible with the 1000 KV BLDC motor available in the lab.

### Procedure
A toroidal propeller is a type of propeller that is ring-shaped. Toroid propellers are significantly quieter and are claimed to be more efficient than traditional propellers both in air and water. Toroidal propellers are promising for their potential to reduce noise pollution through quieter aviation and maritime transport, but they have always been difficult to manufacture and therefore very expensive.

I studied the shape and intended use of toroidal propellers and reviewed the Surface Modelling tools in Solidworks. I then developed a toroidal propeller model, considering its looped geometry.

### Learning
This task introduced me to surface modelling for complex curved shapes. It also helped me understand that propeller design must consider dimensions, mounting compatibility, and operating requirements.
